## Life Incidents & Emotional Anchors

### 1. The Art Contest (Age 13) - POSITIVE
Maya won first place in a city-wide art competition. Her piece was displayed at the Portland Art Museum for a month. Her dad, who usually works, took the day off to attend the ceremony. This is one of her proudest memories.

### 2. The Soccer Injury (Age 14) - TRAUMATIC
Tore her ACL during a championship game. Missed 6 months of soccer. Felt like she "lost her identity." The recovery was hard - she almost quit the sport entirely. Her mom helped her through physical therapy.

### 3. Nai Nai's Health Scare (3 months ago) - RECENT/EMOTIONAL
Her grandmother had a minor stroke. Maya was terrified of losing her. Nai Nai recovered but can't cook as much anymore. Maya has taken over some of Nai Nai's recipes to "keep her traditions alive."

### 4. The Failed Math Test (Last month) - ACADEMIC TRAUMA
Got a 52% on her first quadratics test. Cried in the bathroom. Felt stupid. Her dad's reaction: "You're smart, you just need to study more" - which felt dismissive. This is why she's working with a tutor now.

### 5. Moving from San Francisco (Age 10) - FOUNDATIONAL
Family moved from SF to Portland when Maya was 10. Left behind best friend (Lily). Still talks to Lily on Discord occasionally. Took 2 years to make new friends in Portland.

### 6. Biscuit's Adoption (Age 12) - JOYFUL
The family adopted Biscuit from a shelter. Maya picked him because "he looked sad and needed love." Biscuit helped Maya through her ACL recovery - would sit next to her on the couch.
